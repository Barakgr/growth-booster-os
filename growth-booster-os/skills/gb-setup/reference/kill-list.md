# The kill list

Every skill that drafts words (`/gb-write`, `/gb-followup`, `/gb-brief` when it suggests replies) scans the draft against this list before showing it, then against the owner's own banned words in `about-me/voice.md` Part 1. Any sentence that trips gets rewritten, not flagged. This same list is copied word for word into `voice.md` Part 2 during setup, so the owner can see exactly what Claude avoids.

Banned openers: Great question. Absolutely. Certainly. I'd be happy to help. Let's dive in. In today's fast-paced world. I hope this email finds you well.
Banned filler: It's worth noting that. It's important to mention. One thing to keep in mind. Ultimately. In essence. At the end of the day. That said. Please don't hesitate to reach out.
Banned corporate-speak: Leverage (as a verb). Synergy. Deep dive. Circle back. Touch base. Move the needle. Unlock. Empower. Journey (as an abstraction). Game-changer. Holistic. Robust. Seamless. Elevate. Best-in-class. World-class.
Banned marketing hype (Growth Booster house rule): Guaranteed results. Explode your business. Dominate your market. Set it and forget it. AI magic. Revolutionary. Effortless. We care about our customers. We go above and beyond.
Banned structural tells: Three bullet points that aren't really a list. First/Second/Third when the items aren't sequential. Closing with "I hope this helps!" or "Let me know if you have any questions." Opening with an em-dash flourish. A rhetorical question as the first line.
Banned tone tells: Flattering the reader in the first sentence. Over-qualifying ("This is just my opinion, but..."). Explaining a simple word choice. Apologizing for the length.
Rule: before showing any draft, scan it against this list and the owner's own banned words in voice.md Part 1. Rewrite any sentence that trips. The owner's voice always wins over this list — the list is the floor, not the ceiling.
